## Decisions that require user review before implementation

1. **Promotion status policy.** Proposed default: OPEN/INVESTIGATING hold; RESOLVED/ACCEPTED_BOTH/SUPERSEDED do not themselves hold, while all other evidence/trust gates still apply. Public `dismissed` is an alias for stored SUPERSEDED (`embers/integration/conflict_protocol.py:18-23`; `tests/test_agent_conflict.py:59-66`). Any active mapped conflict among several relationships holds. Decide whether a graph-only CONTRADICTS edge without a mapped CONFLICT remains a conservative hold or routes to review. Decide whether explicit Human `db.promote` may override an active conflict, and how missing/unreadable derivation records are handled. `features update` establishes the engines and review modes but does not settle these cross-engine outcomes. No policy branch should be coded before approval.
2. **Integrity scope and authority.** Decide who may run/repair versus read findings, whether whole-store scans disclose cross-namespace IDs, how to classify legacy unsealed records, and retention/redaction of diagnostic findings. `/maintenance` authentication alone is not namespace authorization (`embers/api/feedback_routes.py:76-87`). Index drift is repairable derived metadata; source corruption is a separate diagnostic.
3. **Index trust boundary.** Choose fail-closed behavior for unreadable unrelated source during initial certification and out-of-band byte edits between audits. The proposed warm check catches ID/sidecar changes and in-process metadata drift, while a same-ID physical byte edit to an old unrelated record needs audit or a stronger proof. Do not claim more than the selected contract proves.
4. **ELLA historical-integrity boundary.** Choose the maximum allowed detection delay for out-of-band modification of an old event after warm replay, and whether failed/overdue audit forces replay or blocks accelerated reads. Per-request proof of all old event bytes entails a source scan or another trusted proof, contrary to the target replay saving. This tradeoff must be explicit.
5. **Protocol presentation.** Decide how stale conflict status and preexisting ambiguous forks are represented to REST/MCP clients while preserving existing response shape where possible. A conflict-specific 409-style response is proposed, but source currently exposes mixed validation/concurrency errors.

The first implementation batch can be prepared against existing semantics, but policy-dependent branches in Batches 2–4 remain gated by these decisions. Approval of the roadmap is not approval to edit source under the Atlas maintenance contract.
